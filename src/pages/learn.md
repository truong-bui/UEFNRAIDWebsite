# Introduction
<p>
RAID is an objective-based third-person shooter created in Fortnite. Two opposing teams, the "Defender" and the "Raider," compete against each other. The Raider's objective is to plant a bomb, while the Defender aims to stop them. Players acquire weapons through a weapon shop UI that appears at the start of the game. There are 51 selected weapons and items in RAID. RAID features Kill Achievement and Ranking System.
</p>

# Game-play overview and general rules
  - Each round last 150 seconds, plus 45 seconds if the bomb is planted by the Raider.
  - Raider's objectives: Plant the bomb at bombsite A or B. Or eliminate all Defenders before time out.
  - Defender's objectives: Defends bombsites A and B, defuse the bomb if it is planted by the Raider, or eliminate all the Raiders.
  - Each team receives 1000 gold each round. The winning team gets an extra 2000 gold. The player receives 300 gold for each elimination.
  - Player acquires weapons through weapon shop UI that appears at start of the game.
