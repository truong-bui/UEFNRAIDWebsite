## We're live

RAID is now playable on the public Fortnite Discover board. Search the island code from the home page to drop in.

## What shipped

- **One full map** — Market, with two bomb sites and a contested mid corridor.
- **Round-based 5v5** with a buy phase, economy carry-over, and side-swap at half.
- **Custom weapon set** spanning rifles, SMGs, snipers, shotguns, pistols, and utility (smokes, flashes, frags).
- **Match scoreboards** with per-round economy tracking and end-of-match MVP highlights.

## What's next

- Second map, codenamed *Foundry*, in late playtest.
- Ranked ladder with seasonal resets.
- Custom-game lobbies for tournament organizers.
