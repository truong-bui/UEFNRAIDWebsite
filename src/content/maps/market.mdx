## Overview

Market is RAID's launch map. The layout is a tight, partially-covered marketplace built around two bomb sites — **A** at the food court on the south end, **B** at the loading dock on the north end. The two sides connect through a long, mostly-open mid corridor that rewards smokes, flashes, and patient rotations.

Defenders spawn closer to A; attackers spawn near mid. Site rotations take roughly 12 seconds without contest, so committing utility to mid is often the difference between holding and losing a round.

## Callouts

- **A-site (Food Court)** — Stalls and counters provide head-glitch angles. Default plant is behind the central counter.
- **B-site (Loading Dock)** — Wide-open with crates as cover. Strong for post-plant trades.
- **Mid (Plaza)** — A long sightline split by an awning. Wallbangs are real; pre-aim chest height.
- **Connector** — Short L-shaped hallway from mid to B. Defender's flashbang lane.
- **Heaven** — Elevated walkway above A. Strong AWP/sniper position.

## Tips

- Smoke mid before any A → B rotate.
- Defenders should stagger holds — one player heaven, one player site, one floater.
- Attackers default plant favors B; A executes need utility commitment.
